Privacy & data use

Datavyx, LLC operates Datavyx. This policy explains how we handle your knowledge, account information and website activity.

Effective November 26, 2026. Contact support@datavyx.com for support and privacy requests.

Your personal space stays separate

Other users cannot access your personal records simply by connecting to Datavyx. Every read and write is checked against your identity, permissions and current workspace membership.

Your chat is not saved automatically

Datavyx receives the source text and structured parameters your AI assistant sends for a tool request, not your complete chat history automatically. We may store submitted originals, objects, facts, relationships, current and previous corrected values, deletion state, request identifiers and operation receipts.

How we use information

We use this information to provide recording, retrieval, exact calculations, corrections, recoverable deletion, restoration and history, and to protect and support the service. We process authenticated identity identifiers, granted permissions and space memberships to control access. Support messages are used to resolve your requests.

Datavyx’s knowledge backend connects directly to its database and does not send your records to an internal language-model, embedding or reranking service. Your calling AI provider is a separate service with its own data practices. We do not sell knowledge records or provide them to advertising services.

No unauthorized access or use

Operational access is limited to what is needed to provide the service, investigate a reported problem, protect security, meet a legal obligation or handle a verified privacy request. Do not send passwords, API keys, access tokens, payment-card details, government identifiers or protected health information.

Service providers and locations

Our knowledge service and its local backups are hosted on AWS infrastructure in the United States. Auth0 provides login and authorization, Google Workspace handles support email, and Vercel hosts this public website. These providers process relevant data to deliver those services; identity, email and website processing may occur in other countries under the providers’ service arrangements.

The AI client you choose, including ChatGPT, receives the requests and tool results involved in your use under its own terms and privacy policy. Datavyx cannot delete copies retained by an independent AI client, email sender or shared-space participant through a Datavyx record deletion.

Retention, deletion and account closure

We retain your knowledge, source text and correction history while your Datavyx account remains open. Ordinary deletion removes information from normal results and statistics but remains recoverable during the account lifetime; it is not permanent erasure.

To close your Datavyx account, email support@datavyx.com. We verify control of the affected account and the requested scope, disable Datavyx access, and remove your personal-space content and Datavyx identity bindings from active systems within 30 days after verification. Shared content and other members’ records are not automatically erased with your account.

Disaster-recovery backups rotate on a maximum 30-day retention period. A copy already in a backup expires within that cycle after active-system erasure, ordinarily no later than 60 days after a verified closure request. Closed-account restrictions are reapplied before a restored database is allowed to serve requests.

Minimal technical and security logs are retained for up to 30 days; they do not include source text, full tool parameters or credentials. Necessary support emails and case records are retained for 12 calendar months after the case is closed. We review only the identified closed support case, not an entire mailbox.

We keep a minimal pseudonymous closure marker to prevent an old backup or a new login from reviving a closed account; it does not contain your knowledge or source text. A specific legal preservation obligation may require limited records to be held longer, with restricted access and removal when that obligation ends.

Your choices and requests

Use authorized tools to read sources, correct information, inspect available history, delete recoverably or restore eligible deletions. You may revoke your AI-client connection or request account and privacy help through support@datavyx.com. Disconnecting or uninstalling the plugin does not itself close your Datavyx account or erase stored records.

We may ask for evidence that you control the affected account and clarification of scope. This protects other users and is separate from ordinary knowledge changes, which execute directly without a Datavyx approval card. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, or receive a copy of your information; contact us to exercise applicable rights. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Website analytics & your choices

With your permission, Microsoft Clarity uses cookies to measure visits, clicks, scrolling and session replays on this public website. Microsoft processes this usage data. We do not send account identifiers, email addresses or knowledge records to Clarity. You can decline or withdraw permission through Analytics preferences. Declining does not prevent you from using this website.

A separate OpenAI Ads Pixel on our homepage measures advertising-related page views. The Clarity preference does not control that pixel. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement, Clarity Terms of Use and OpenAI Privacy Policy. Website analytics are separate from the knowledge database and account authorization.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when these practices change and show its effective date. For questions or privacy requests, contact Datavyx, LLC at support@datavyx.com. See our Support page and Terms of Service.
