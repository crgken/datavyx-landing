Privacy & data use

Your records stay in your personal workspace. Every read and write is checked against your identity and permissions.

Your personal space stays separate

Other users cannot access your personal records simply by connecting to Datavyx.

Access follows your authorization

Your assistant can only read or change records allowed by your granted permissions and workspace membership.

Your chat is not saved automatically

Datavyx receives the information your assistant sends for a tool request, not your complete chat history automatically.

No unauthorized access or use

We do not view or use your information without your permission.

Website analytics & your choices

With your permission, Microsoft Clarity uses cookies to measure visits, clicks, scrolling and session replays on this public website. Microsoft processes this usage data. We do not send account identifiers, email addresses or knowledge records to Clarity. You can decline or withdraw permission through Analytics preferences. Declining does not prevent you from using this website.

A separate OpenAI Ads Pixel on our homepage measures advertising-related page views. The Clarity preference does not control that pixel. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement, Clarity Terms of Use and OpenAI Privacy Policy. For questions, contact support@datavyx.com.
