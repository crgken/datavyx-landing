A few practical details

Before you connect.

What gets saved, how access works, and what happens when information changes.

Where do I use Datavyx?

In an AI assistant with a supported, authorized MCP connection. ChatGPT is verified in production. Other clients must be checked for protocol, OAuth, and account availability.

Is everything in my chat saved automatically?

No. Your assistant sends the specific information and sources needed for a tool request. Datavyx does not receive your complete chat history automatically. Ask your assistant to save what you want to keep.

Can another user see my personal records?

Connecting to the same server does not grant access to your personal workspace. Every operation checks the authenticated identity, granted permissions, and current workspace membership.

What happens when two sources disagree?

Independent conflicting statements retain their sources. Datavyx does not choose a winner just because one arrived later. Relevant exact calculations exclude conflicting values; an explicit correction preserves the previous value and its history.

Can I undo a deletion?

Ordinary deletion is recoverable. Deleted information is excluded from normal reads and can be restored with the required permissions. Permanent erasure is not currently offered.

Will I be asked to confirm every change?

Datavyx directly executes valid, authorized requests and returns a receipt. Your AI client may still show its own tool permission prompts. When a target is ambiguous or required information is missing, your assistant needs to clarify it first.
