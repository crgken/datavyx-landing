Datavyx support

Product support and privacy requests for Datavyx, operated by Datavyx, LLC.

Contact us

Email support@datavyx.com. Please include the AI client you use, the time of the problem, and its error message. Remove private information from screenshots. Do not send passwords, Client Secrets, API keys, access tokens or complete authentication headers. We do not need your whole chat history to investigate a connection problem.

Connection help

Use the Datavyx connection guide on our homepage. A supported AI client signs you in with OAuth. We are preparing a shared public plugin with a preconfigured client so end users will not need to create an OAuth application or share a Client Secret. Contact support for current connection options. Availability depends on the AI platform and your plan or workspace settings.

If a request has an uncertain result, ask the assistant to retrieve the original request receipt before trying to save it again. Tell support the safe request identifier if available, without sending authentication data.

Correct, delete or restore information

Authorized plugin tools can correct information with history, delete it recoverably and restore eligible deletions. If a target is ambiguous or a selection is incomplete, clarify which records you mean. Datavyx does not require a separate approval card for valid knowledge operations. Your AI client can still display its own permission prompts.

Ordinary deletion is recoverable, and the public tools do not offer permanent erasure. A record removed from current results is not a promise that every backup copy has been erased.

Account closure and privacy requests

To close your Datavyx account, email support@datavyx.com. We verify control of the affected account and the requested scope, disable Datavyx access, and remove your personal-space content and Datavyx identity bindings from active systems within 30 days after verification. Shared content and other members’ records are not automatically erased with your account.

Disaster-recovery backups rotate on a maximum 30-day retention period. A copy already in a backup expires within that cycle after active-system erasure, ordinarily no later than 60 days after a verified closure request. Closed-account restrictions are reapplied before a restored database is allowed to serve requests.

Necessary support case records are retained for 12 calendar months after case closure. See the Privacy policy for the full retention rules and the Terms of Service.
